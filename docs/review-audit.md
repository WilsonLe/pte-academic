# Saved question review audit

Issue [#46](https://github.com/WilsonLe/pte-academic/issues/46) covers all 65 original questions and 22 types. Daily, question-type, section and full-mock sessions share `Review.tsx`; unfinished sessions use the same comparisons.

## Behavior and content

- Choices retain every option with selected, correct, incorrect and missed labels. Saved selections appear first, then missed keys, then other unselected options, preserving their original option letters. Numbered gaps retain empty positions and show a differing key inline. Paragraph order preserves original source Paragraph IDs while feedback uses Position N for the answer order; selected explanations refer explicitly to neighbouring positions in the answer. Identical answers omit a duplicate key.
- Incorrect-word review identifies each selected or missed word by its source position and shows the written and heard forms. Dictation compares the saved sentence without estimating exam credit; an equal normalized opening stays matched before aligning repeated words in the remainder.
- Writing gives the observed word count and distance from the allowed range. Content and language remain self-review. Summary examples are alternatives; essay support is explicitly a planning outline.
- All 65 explanations were read against their original prompt/key and rewritten to identify the relevant evidence, grammatical form, contrast or self-review target. Five chart examples now describe the data with units; two lecture examples are complete spoken responses, and the room-booking example asks for the room. Prompts, source scripts, answer keys, charts and audio are unchanged.
- Issue [#50](https://github.com/WilsonLe/pte-academic/issues/50) supersedes the original panel design: every question uses one Feedback surface. The original question is visible beside the response (stacked on narrow screens), with measured passages, numbered gaps/paragraphs and inline charts. Learner playback and response/key comparisons are integrated; skipped questions are filtered and zero/single-response sessions omit the question pager.
- All seven speaking types use local audio-only word recognition followed by experimental word-local syllable comparison; optional prompt differences are display-only. Word selection opens listening and local syllable practice. A completed retry earns 5 effort XP once per target/day without changing the original response, grade, quest or streak. Missing audio/reference, uncertain and no-speech states remain distinct; retained legacy results stay readable.
- Shared `ReadingPages` measures labelled blocks, including headings, line breaks and continued text, against the actual font and available panel. It retains one content pager, preserves long responses, and keeps the short-window scrolling escape. Existing plain-text consumers retain deduplication and packed reading pages.
- Issue [#61](https://github.com/WilsonLe/pte-academic/issues/61) keeps writing as one neutral response panel, paged against its actual rendered size with paragraph boundaries preserved. The single Details disclosure owns question-specific checks and the writing alternative or essay planning outline; objective comparisons retain their selectable cards. Focused regressions cover all writing types, long/enlarged text, reload and keyboard dismissal.

The #61 visible-browser retest saved the same 241-word essay, read every measured page, reopened it through History and reloaded it, then exercised Details and its planning outline. Mobile review uses five measured pages instead of 14 response cards across seven pages. It also verified the [single writing panel](evidence/playtest-61-essay-mobile.png), [explicit section/question clocks](evidence/playtest-61-timers-mobile.png), and [expanded mobile title](evidence/playtest-61-title-mobile.png), including Escape/focus return and short-window contained scrolling. This is local static-preview evidence; full mock completion, real microphone inference and deployment were outside this change's retest.

## Verification

`tests/review.spec.ts` enumerates source IDs directly. For all 390 question/state/viewport combinations, it reads every page of the response, Feedback and source panels; checks expected source/key/example content, readable panel geometry and page completion; and inspects charts and source-audio controls. It also checks mixed mock/section navigation, keyboard selection, panel fallback, reload, long responses at 320×568, enlarged text in a short window, source replay and playback cleanup.

`tests/review.test.ts` checks every content ID plus partial choices, duplicate selections, unanswered gap positions, incomplete ordering, written/heard positions, writing limits, and dictation case/punctuation/order. Existing speech journeys cover saved grades, uncertain/no-speech results, missing recordings, empty/legacy transcripts, model-not-ready controls, playback and reload. Existing focused practice journeys cover actual saved attempts and all 65 mock review items. Shared reading consumers are checked through every guide, learning topic, section and mock details.

Builds validate content, pinned comparison assets, TypeScript, static-only publishing and the 500 kB chunk budget. Storage/comparison unit contracts stay in the focused selection. Full-suite and hosted release evidence remain reserved for merge authority; native iOS/Safari and screen-reader behavior are not established by Chromium checks. No model-pack, storage, reward, credential, paid-call or dependency change is included. Rollback is a normal revert.

Current captures: [desktop prompt diff](evidence/feedback-prompt-desktop.png), [mobile prompt diff](evidence/feedback-prompt-mobile.png), and [selected-word practice](evidence/feedback-word-practice.png). Real pinned-model browser journeys separately verify retry feedback, rewards, microphone lifecycle, delayed initial-result refresh and missing/corrupt-cache recovery; synthetic audio still needs human listening review.

The operator's colour revision renders green/yellow/red directly on authored syllable letter groups, retaining the original word text and punctuation. Uncertain groups stay neutral and the prompt has no number/question-mark chips. Thresholds remain 75/50, with detailed scores available in word practice and accessible descriptions. All current fixed-speech model variants have checked spelling coverage; unsupported future groups remain neutral.

Independent-word revision: the operator chose audio-only recognition for speaking activities without text, and the existing synthetic references rather than a human-verified gold corpus. Speaking review now derives words/times without a prompt, then grades word-local syllable sounds with v3; optional prompt differences are display-only. Word dialogs use learner intervals plus a global synthetic word library (finite coverage, explicit missing clips). Open tasks use the same path and effort rewards. Legacy prompt grades are retained during upgrade. See training/WORD_RECOGNITION.md for exact manifests, regeneration and validation limits. The prior independent review predates this model integration; no new automatic review cycle is implied.

## Quiet review and aligned columns (#136, #137)

Question and response/Feedback headers share a 48px minimum row and a 10px content gap. Query choices use the actual query as the source heading instead of an extra row. Headers may grow; source text is never clipped to force alignment. Summarize Spoken Text keeps its single replayable source player beneath Response. Charts, numbered paragraphs/gaps, word positions, complete measured writing and selected/missed/uncertain labels remain intact. Phone layouts stack the same panels and retain contained scrolling.

Speaking coverage and the complete reference-similarity limitation open through existing Details. Selected-word practice keeps audio, syllable similarity, Record and status visible; its inline Practice details holds instructions/reward rules, synthetic unverified-reference provenance, valid accents, approximate timing, local retry limits and sound notation. Closing either disclosure restores focus. These are display changes, with no grading or reward change.
